\begin{frame}{Mathematics}

  Consider a total system cost minimisation problem\footnote{Assume the investment costs are annualised.}:

  \begin{equation*}
    \min_{g_{s,t}, G_s}\overbrace{\sum_s c_s G_s}^{\text{investment costs}} + \overbrace{\sum_{s,t} o_{s} g_{s,t}}^{\text{fuel costs}},   \qquad \text{s.t.}
  \end{equation*}
  \begin{align*}
    \sum_s g_{s,t} - d_t =  0 &\hspace{0.34cm}\perp \hspace{0.34cm} \lambda_t \tag{nodal energy balance}
  \end{align*}

  The dual variable $\lambda_t$ of the energy balance is the \alert{market price}.

\end{frame}
